The conference

Diplomacy, rehearsed for the real thing.

IMMUN’2026, organised by Igniting Minds, is a new Model United Nations conference taking place on September 5–6, 2026. It will give students the opportunity to experience diplomacy, debate global issues, negotiate, build alliances, and develop leadership and communication skills. More than a conference, IMMUN aims to create lasting friendships, meaningful experiences, and a community of confident, curious young leaders.
